About

Designing for the edges. Building for resilience.

I'm Edgar Bonilla G., a UI/UX designer specializing in digital health, wellness, lifestyle and fitness. I believe that good design in healthcare isn't just about aesthetics — it's about clarity, accessibility, and trust.

Over the past few years, I've focused on creating interfaces that work for people in vulnerable or high-stress states. Whether it's a senior trying to navigate a post-op recovery portal, or an endurance athlete checking their stats immediately after a grueling swim, the interface needs to be resilient.

My approach is evidence-disciplined. I rely on research, accessibility standards (WCAG 2.2 AA is my baseline), and a deep understanding of the user's physical and cognitive context. I design for stories that make it easy to understand the problem, the decisions, and the evidence — building designs that are inclusive, robust, and beautifully functional.
